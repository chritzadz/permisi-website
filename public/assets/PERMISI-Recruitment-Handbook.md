## Slide 2: What is PERMISI?
**About PERMISI**
- **PERMISI** = Persatuan Mahasiswa Indonesia CityU
- Indonesian Students' Association CityU
- "Permisi" means "excuse me" - representing our respectful culture
- Making our presence known while staying true to our roots

## Slide 3: Our Vision & Mission
